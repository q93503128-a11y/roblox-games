# Slay & Loot — Vision

## Product identity

Slay & Loot is a Roblox action-growth game where the player directly fights groups of monsters, gets visible equipment drops, becomes dramatically stronger, and pushes into stronger encounters and zones.

The game should feel easy to understand, fast to start, and satisfying to grow in. It should not depend on a long tutorial or a large open world to become fun.

## Stable pillars

1. **Immediate action** — the player reaches meaningful combat quickly.
2. **Visible growth** — better gear should look and feel stronger, not only raise hidden numbers.
3. **Group combat** — the player should regularly fight multiple enemies, not only isolated one-by-one targets.
4. **Loot desire** — rare drops, equipment silhouettes, variants, and strong feedback should make players want one more run/kill.
5. **Compact progression** — zones are dense and purposeful; map size is not a quality metric.
6. **Acceleration** — later progression and rebirth should make earlier content collapse faster and feel meaningfully conquered.
7. **Roblox readability** — controls, silhouettes, rewards, and progression must remain understandable on desktop and mobile.

## Development philosophy

Do not fully pre-design every weapon, monster, zone, animation, and VFX before seeing what high-quality assets and references are actually available.

Use this loop:

`direction -> reference/asset search -> implementation -> playtest -> accept/reject -> update live design`

The project documentation should become more precise as tested content becomes real.

## Non-goals

At the start, Slay & Loot is not:

- a giant open-world quest RPG
- a dialogue-heavy story RPG
- a Soulslike
- a full Vampire Survivors clone
- a click-only simulator
- a pet-collection-first game
- a complex trading economy
- a content-count race

## Quality priority

`runs correctly -> is safe -> is fun/usable -> looks coherent -> is maintainable -> has more content`

A small good slice is preferred over a large unfinished game.
