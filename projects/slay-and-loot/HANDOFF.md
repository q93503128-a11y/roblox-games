## Current confirmed direction

Slay & Loot is a Roblox action-growth game focused on:

- direct combat against groups of monsters
- equipment/loot as the main visible growth driver
- compact zone progression
- strong power acceleration over time
- rebirth/progression later, after the core combat-and-loot loop proves fun
- active use of audited external models, animations, VFX, and environment kits when they improve quality and speed

Exact art/content is intentionally allowed to evolve from real asset availability and playtest results.
